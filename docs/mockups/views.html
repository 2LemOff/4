3. The Mechanism: “Each human has an actuator… thanks to which human moves”

  • The Principle: Human movement requires a physical mechanism transferring energy.
  • The Scrutiny: Your “actuators” are biological motors (muscles, nerves, ATP molecules). From a first-principles physics perspective, movement is just the transfer of potential chemical energy into kinetic energy via electromagnetism. The particles in your brain send electrical signals to the particles in your muscles, causing protein filaments (myosin and actin) to bind and pull against each other.
  • Verdict: Strictly true. Human agency and physical movement are entirely the result of microscopic particle interactions governed by electromagnetic forces.

Where does the energy in ATP come from?
‹ 1 / 2 › · other branch: Why EM, not gravity?

From food. Your cells break glucose and fats apart using oxygen. The energy released pumps protons across the inner membrane of the mitochondria.

As the protons flow back through ATP synthase, a tiny rotating motor, it rebuilds ATP from ADP.

Ultimately that energy came from sunlight that plants captured by photosynthesis, so your movement runs on stored sunlight.
